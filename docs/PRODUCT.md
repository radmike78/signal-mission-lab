# Product requirements and acceptance criteria

## Core learner journeys
1. **Mission**: choose a fictional exercise; identify echelon, supported organizations, information-exchange goals, assumptions and responsibilities; generate an educational requirements review.
2. **Equipment**: search cross-branch systems, explore purpose, dependencies, support roles and knowledge gaps; never assume IP connectivity establishes interoperability.
3. **Architecture**: explore and edit a **synthetic** service-dependency model; no operational topologies or connection parameters.
4. **Troubleshoot**: diagnose synthetic faults with selectable checks, reveal evidence and root cause, record progress locally.
5. **Doctrine**: navigate MDMP, ADM, TLP and staff concepts with publication identifiers; publication editions and source content require human validation.

## Content quality
- Each future factual technical record requires a source URL, publication edition, review date, reviewer and releasability check.
- Mark overview-only records as unverified; do not fabricate interface specifications, technical compatibility, equipment allocations or integration steps.
- Explain equipment ownership versus Signal support responsibilities.
- All user text remains local to browser in this prototype; do not paste sensitive content.

## Deferred production work
- Reviewed primary-source ingestion and citation-backed search.
- Authentication, roles, database, moderation, audit and instructor tools.
- Actual AI mentor only after safe retrieval and source quality controls.
- Versioned scenario engine and assessment analytics.
- Accessible diagram editor, automated tests, deployment pipeline and security review.
