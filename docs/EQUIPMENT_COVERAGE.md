# Army tactical equipment coverage register

19 October 2026 UTC. Coverage is **incomplete**. Entries include equipment families, individual designations, software, acquisition models, infrastructure and editorial study categories. They are not 83 distinct fielded hardware items.

## Scope and evidence

Expanded from seven catalog overviews to 83 entries. Evidence states: source_checked: 59, retrieval_pending: 8, overview_only: 16. Source-checked means public purpose checked against retrieved primary content; human review, current fielding, exact variants and interfaces remain unverified. Discovery-only entries are not used as evidence claims.

Upper TI / Lower TI are editorial browse areas. Modern converged networks and cross-tier capabilities do not have a universal boundary. Area labels do not assert connectivity or doctrinal assignment. Dependencies and coordination roles are investigation prompts unless individually sourced.

The mission builder supports explicit catalog selection (maximum 12). Its local interpreter recognizes selected common acronyms and designations, not every alias or variant. Missing fields still prompt clarification. Explicit transport choices prevent automatically adding legacy JNN/CPN comparison categories. The existing JNN / WIN-T label is retained for compatibility; separate Increment 1 and 2 program records distinguish the family from a node.

## Access limitations and remaining work

Program-office TNT-ATH, SATCOM FoT and PRC-160 pages returned 403; historical Rifleman/Manpack and PSC-5 training pages returned 429; the TRC-190 article timed out. Their discovery records remain retrieval_pending. No restricted manuals or inventory were requested. Existing FM 5-0 retrieval remains pending.

An approved public program inventory and releasable variant-specific references are needed to substantiate exhaustiveness. No unit property book, operational architecture, keys, fills or COMSEC material should be submitted.

Remaining gaps:
- Exact LIN/NSN and variant coverage requires an approved public authoritative inventory; no unit property books requested.
- HF, narrowband TACSAT, retransmission, mounted integration kits and legacy radio variants require additional public references.
- Tropo, older multichannel assemblages, strategic/enterprise transport and baseband variants require additional public references.
- SCN/MCN/LCN-T variant taxonomy, other newer SATCOM terminal families require full-source retrieval and review.
- Routers, switches, servers, fiber/copper, antennas, power and environmental support need public variant-level references.
- Public cryptographic-device purpose coverage is pending; no keys, fills, COMSEC procedures or material accepted.
- Commercial cellular, private 5G, edge/cloud, UNO and emerging transport require current-source review.
- All current fielding, lifecycle and interface assertions need source-specific human review.

## Validation and security

Catalog tests enforce unique IDs, one-to-one metadata, generated mission option consistency, primary-source hosts, pending-source exclusion and coverage of both browse areas. Model tests cover designation matching, negation, selection bounds and transport choice preservation. Browser checks exercise filters, pending-source display, named mission choices, XSS-like search text and mobile layout. Full CI validates the application, accounts, dependencies and container.

Only fixed catalog identifiers are accepted for mission selection. No additional free-text submission, AI service or credential is introduced. React renders source content as text; primary links are static HTTPS records. The evidence register is exposed alongside the API graph without changing existing fields.
