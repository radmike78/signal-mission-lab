# Signal Products learning reference

## Behavior

Signal Products is a named navigation destination under More learning tools. The three primary destinations remain Mission, Troubleshoot and My training. Learners choose a staff or Signal-unit perspective, then one product. The reference explains purpose, preparer/coordination roles, users and update timing before optional review prompts, a fictional example, common mistakes and source notes. Changing the product closes optional detail. The network-diagram example represents conceptual dependencies only.

## Coverage

18 entries: Annex H; SOI; Signal running estimate; diagrams supporting Annex H; PACE; synchronization/support timeline; requirements/support requests; communications status/Signal COP; communications rehearsal plan/results; site readiness; task organization/support responsibilities; service acceptance/shift handover; SOP/TACSOP; battle-drill/incident checklist; risk/assumptions/decisions; equipment/sustainment readiness; spectrum coordination approval tracking; and after-action improvement records.

These are relevant learning categories, not a universal mandatory product list for every S6, G6, company or ESB. Doctrine-context entries are distinguished from Army lessons-learned context and editorial worksheets. Suggested contents, examples, role descriptions and review prompts are educational interpretations. Actual format, approval, ownership and reporting requirements follow applicable doctrine, orders and unit SOP. In particular, a tracking worksheet is not an authorization or official submission form.

## Security and validation

The page is static and read-only, with fixed selectors and static primary-source links. It has no document entry, uploads, operational-data fields, generated operational templates, external fetches, persistence, credentials, device actions or model calls. React renders text without raw HTML. SOI coverage explains purpose and edition control without supplying communications or COMSEC data. Diagram lines do not establish equipment interoperability. The new module loads on demand to keep the homepage bundle from absorbing the new reference.

The offline source gate checks unique products/sources, required teaching fields, fixed evidence/audience labels, public Army URL origins and reviewed evidence. Tests reject spoofed hosts, missing/unknown evidence, unread-source claims, duplicate products and unsupported operational-approval labels. Browser coverage checks product selection, disclosure reset, perspective filtering, SOI scope, conceptual diagram, desktop/mobile layout and absence of upload controls. CI captures signal-products.png, signal-product-diagram.png and mobile-signal-products.png.

Production readiness is not established. Organizational authentication, host deployment validation and the previously documented release requirements remain outstanding.
