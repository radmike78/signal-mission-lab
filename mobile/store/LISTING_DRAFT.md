# Store listing drafts — owner approval required

Name: Signal Mission Lab

Short description: Mission-first Signal learning and troubleshooting, available offline.

Description:
Practice turning a training mission into a reasoned service concept. Learn what Signal systems and mission command applications are for, examine dependencies, and work through synthetic recovery exercises one check at a time.

- Guided mission questions and educational recommendations
- MDMP prompts, Signal products, public source references and equipment study cards
- Interactive dependency diagrams and IT foundations
- PowerShell builder, routing/tunnel and satellite recovery reasoning exercises
- Formative assessments and up to 50 practice results on your device
- Bundled lessons and exercises, usable without a connection

Public reference websites need internet access. This edition has no accounts or instructor server connection. Practice scores do not certify competence. Equipment study cards do not establish interoperability, provide operational configurations or replace approved technical manuals. Unofficial education; no Army endorsement. Do not enter classified information, CUI, COMSEC or operational plans.

## Reviewer notes
No login or backend is required. In airplane mode, start at Mission or Troubleshoot; choose an issue, inspect checks, select a recovery decision, then visit My training. More learning tools contains assessments, diagrams, Signal products and Offline & privacy. Links to original public sources open a separate browser and are optional. All training outcomes are simulations.
