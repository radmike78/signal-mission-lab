# Signal Mission Lab mobile privacy policy — release draft

Effective date: set at publication. Owner, support email and public policy URL: **must be supplied before submission**. This draft describes the offline mobile edition, not the optional instructor website.

The app bundles public educational material and invented training exercises. It does not require an account. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, remote AI model, or automatic practice upload. It requests no camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

The app saves up to 50 formative practice results (activity category, score, possible score and completion time) in operating-system preferences. Mission descriptions and access credentials are not saved in this practice history. Use **My training → Clear device practice** to erase results. Operating-system backups may contain preferences according to device settings; Android app backup is disabled. Device storage is not a repository for sensitive information.

Reference links open the user's separate browser when selected and require a connection. Those websites and browser providers have their own privacy policies. The bundled app does not download reference manuals or make its own server requests. Opening the optional instructor website is a separate service with separate account and activity processing; it is not included in the mobile edition.

Do not enter classified information, CUI, COMSEC, credentials or real operational plans. This is unofficial educational software, with no Army endorsement. It is not certified for operational or classified use.

No app data is sold or shared by the developer. Contact the owner through the published support address for questions. This statement and store disclosures must be re-reviewed if telemetry, accounts, remote content, payments or AI are added.
